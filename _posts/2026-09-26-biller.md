Most invoice tools arrive with a decision to make before you have written a
single line. Pick a plan. Create an account. Choose a template from four
hundred. Connect a payment processor. Then fill in the form.

You wanted to send an invoice.

Biller is at [biller.zerofy.me](https://biller.zerofy.me), and it does the
opposite. The workspace is three numbered sections and you can finish one
without signing up for anything.

## Three sections, in order

The whole editor is a single scrolling page, and it is ordered the way you
actually do the job.

**01 / Details** is you and the invoice. Your name, your invoice number, your
address, the date, the due date, and the currency. Five currencies ship today —
USD, EUR, GBP, INR and AUD — because picking one should not be a reason to close
the tab.

**02 / Recipient** is the customer. Name, email, address. That is the whole
section, because it is the only part your client will actually read.

**03 / Line items** is the work. Description, quantity, price, amount, and an
`Add item` button when you have more than one. Below that sit a tax rate, a
discount amount, and a free-text notes field.

Then it totals itself. Subtotal, tax, total due, recalculating as you type, with
a live preview of the printed page beside it.

## The first ten invoices are free

Here is the part that surprised us to build. You do not need an account to
start. Biller tracks a guest quota of ten invoices, and only when you are about
to run out does it ask who you are.

Past that it is ₹99 a month, or ₹999 a year, and the account is what unlocks
unlimited invoices, invoice history, custom header names, and removing the
watermark from the free tier. The watermark stays on free invoices because the
free tier is a real, working product rather than a trial, and a small mark is a
fairer price than a nag.

If you want the invoice history, you want the free account anyway. It is the
cheapest part of the product.

## The unglamorous parts we kept

**A signature and stamp area.** Because in most of the markets we work in an
invoice without one gets asked about, and the answer should not be a Zoom call.

**Save, preview, clear.** You can save a draft, come back to it, print it as a
PDF, or throw the whole thing away and start clean without hunting for a reset
button first.

**Guest mode is a real mode.** The app tells you plainly that it is guest mode
and that saved invoices will be waiting in history. It does not pretend
otherwise and then lose them.

## Why we built it rather than shipped it

Because we maintain it. Biller is not something we resell or white-label, it is
a tool we needed for our own invoicing and then put a URL on. That means the
people who wrote it are the people who get the bug report, and it also means
the unglamorous work — the guest quota, the stamp area, the print stylesheet —
gets done, because nobody is going to sell us on the value of any of it.

It is also the honest test of everything this Zlog claims. We write about
deployment and keeping a site healthy. Biller is the proof, and it is a real
service on a real domain with real invoices on it.
